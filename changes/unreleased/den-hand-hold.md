While den-live's coordinator directed the Den's virtual spots, a person could no longer aim a spotlight or paint its radial zones: every aim was refused, and the room glow overwrote painted zones at once. Now nothing a person does is refused. The changed fixture is held: the coordinator leaves it alone until den-live has a new reason to act, until Resume, or until the coordinator is turned on again. Each settled change is sent to the coordinator to learn from. The Den chip says "You're in control of" the fixture, with a Resume chip.
