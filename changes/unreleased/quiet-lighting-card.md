---
title: Light commands no longer flash a card in the Home chat
target: web
type: changed
---

While Home switches lights, the chat shows only "Switching lights…" and then
the result. The lighting card that used to flash for a second now stays out of
sight. It appears only when you are needed: when a result could not be
confirmed and needs Check outcome, or when about five seconds pass without an
answer, for example because the Home Agent sign-in expired.
