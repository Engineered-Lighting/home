---
title: A held phone is never called a remote
target: backend
type: fixed
---

When the segmentation pass names a held thing "cell phone" or "remote", den-live keeps its outline as the object's points but sends it unnamed, so Home shows "Held object". A phone in the hand is small in the camera frame, and the pass sometimes called it a remote at the same scores as a held book, so no score floor could separate them. Frames named this way and frames held by depth alone now count together toward the two-of-three hold. Books, cups and the other holdable things keep their names.
