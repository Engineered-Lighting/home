title: Add a loopback den simulation that runs the pipeline tools on rendered takes
target: internal
type: added

Adds `tools/den-sim`, a local harness that serves the unchanged Home on `127.0.0.1`. A rendered, artificial person is recorded once through the real take, check and camera-registration tools, and a real detector, the Lab's YOLO26s and RTMPose-m workers run by the new `tools/den-detect` on this PC's CPU, records its rows beside each take, checked again against the model before the set is used. Each click re-checks that take and its rows by hash, runs the pinned observe, track and bundle tools on the rows, rebuilds each router request's exact Qwen request from the take's own pixels, and looks Qwen's reply up by that request's hash. Qwen's words are recorded once, by the owner's own paste of `tools/den-sim/ask_la.py`, which asks Qwen on the inference host about the requests still owed and files the replies on this PC; nothing on a click reaches the inference host, and until the paste a click stops at the reply with "Qwen has not seen these exact frames", while the page shows what Qwen still owes and the paste line. Need v0 runs on those replies, and the older lighting brain swings a virtual spotlight, with the fixture command shown on a fictional profile and marked not sent. A "Run every scene" button fills a plain table of all five scenes. The panel draws the detector's box and joints on the take's own frame, and shows the stills each Qwen request sends with every field of the reply. Each hop's word comes from a receipt the click writes, and the top line says plainly that the chain is not yet connected start to finish, and nothing has a route to a bulb, the camera, the Jetson or Home Assistant. Its data lives under `%USERPROFILE%\den-sim`, and the detector's venv under `%USERPROFILE%\ml\den-detector`.
