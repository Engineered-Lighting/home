---
title: The den's pipeline fits a phone
target: web
type: changed
---

On a phone, the den's pipeline view shows four stations across the top of the room (camera, den-live, Qwen and the
light), with Frigate's occupancy as a small gate under Qwen. Tapping a station docks its details below the room instead
of over it, and Qwen's card drains a thin line as its answer's time runs out. Turning the pipeline on lowers Home's
sheet so the room has the screen, and turning it off puts the sheet back. Only that toggle moves the sheet: a page load
or coming back to the app with the pipeline left on keeps the height you chose.
