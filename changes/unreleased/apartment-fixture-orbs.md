Each modeled fixture in the room now shows as a small orb with a soft glow tinted by the channels that are on, instead of a white tube with strip segments; an all-off fixture shows only a dim core. Picking, aiming, channel levels, the 160 ms fades and the light it casts are unchanged, and nothing is sent to hardware.
