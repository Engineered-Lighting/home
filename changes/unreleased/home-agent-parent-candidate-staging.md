title: Authenticate dormant parent candidate provenance
target: internal
type: added
---

Adds an offline, non-deployable compiler that re-verifies the signed reviewed
identity bundle and distinct finalization envelope before deriving exactly two
legacy parent-label candidates. It accepts no parent selector and binds their
People, unique receipt, projection, lineage, source, and signed-artifact
commitments while keeping current status, child binding, erasure coverage,
confirmation, database writes, and explicit parent facts disabled. The result
is non-portable unless the original signed artifacts and reconstructed source
rows are re-verified.
