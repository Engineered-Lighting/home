---
title: Pan, pinch and a steadier room view in the Apartment
target: web
type: added
---

In the Apartment room view, Shift-drag or right-drag now pans and a two-finger pinch steps through the zoom detents. The room is framed above the room toolbar. Once you have orbited, zoomed or panned, resizing the window or changing the sheet or dock keeps your view instead of resetting it; Fit and Home still reset it.
