title: Camera questions follow the home you are in
target: web
type: changed
---

Asking the Home chat about your cameras without naming a room or a home, such
as "show me my home" or "what's happening at home?", no longer asks which home
you mean. In Victoria it checks the den and says why, for example "you're in
Victoria"; in Los Angeles it asks only which room. Naming a room or a home
works as before.
