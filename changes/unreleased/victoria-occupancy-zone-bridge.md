title: Victoria den occupancy and zone bridge for the lighting brain
target: backend
type: added
---

A new service turns Frigate's den person counts in Victoria into occupancy, desk
and sofa zone, and Frigate health events for the lighting brain. It sends them on
each change and as regular heartbeats, and it never controls lights. It also
includes a proposal for the den desk and sofa Frigate zones for the owner to
draw. Nothing is deployed yet; deploying the bridge and applying the zones need
the owner's approval.
