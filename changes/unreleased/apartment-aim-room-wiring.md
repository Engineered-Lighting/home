title: Aim Apartment lights from the room through a proposal you Set or Discard
target: desktop
type: changed
---

In the Apartment view, each Den light now has a small label in the room, with a soft ring on hover and a selected ring; the first selection opens the Light tab. Only the 44 px reticle starts an aim, so pressing a light's body selects it without moving its aim or the camera. Dragging the reticle, tapping a surface with Tap to aim, or nudging the focused reticle with the arrow keys (Shift for bigger steps) leaves a cyan proposal while the current beam stays where it was. Its chip names the surface, distance and tilt, and notes that Victoria's aim is modeled from the socket display point and that its travel limits are not commissioned. Set aim (Enter) or Discard (Escape) works from the bar in the room or the Aim tab, with Chat still open and the phone sheet raised to half. Each light has its own 30-step Cmd/Ctrl+Z and Shift+Cmd/Ctrl+Z, and Cmd/Ctrl+Z also brings back a discarded proposal. The Spot button shows a cyan dot while an aim waits, and a Spot or Down brightness slider turns its channel on. Everything stays in the modeled preview and no hardware command is sent.
