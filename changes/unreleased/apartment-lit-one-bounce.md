title: One bounce of light in the Apartment Lit view
target: web
type: added

In Lit, the eight most strongly lit patches (spot targets, down pools and lit walls) now bounce light back into the room, so lighting a wall brightens the space around it. Albedos are assumed. The view states that bounce casts no shadows and can leak up to 4.5 m through walls. The "Toward walls" shortcut waits on the facing-kind decision (APT-Z04).
