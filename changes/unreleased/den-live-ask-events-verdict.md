---
title: den-live asks Qwen as soon as something changes, and Home and the light share one close-up verdict
target: backend
type: changed
---

den-live's Qwen ask no longer waits out its 2-second spacing when something changes. A new seated episode, or the hands or upper body moving 10 cm and then holding still, asks at once, at most one question a second, still one at a time, under the 30-a-minute cap, and never while the back-off monitor slows things. The `event_asks` setting switches this on, and it's off by default until AGENTS.md's den-live section, which says at most once every 2 s today, allows it. The question "is this close-up work?" now has one answer, from one function with the phone, screen and closed-book vetoes, and both Home's Qwen line and the lighting coordinator read it, so Home can no longer say "close-up work" while the light refuses.
