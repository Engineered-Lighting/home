---
title: Den light orbs glow larger the more light they give
target: desktop
type: changed
---

Each modeled Den light's glow now grows with how much light its socket gives, from a small dim glow when one channel is low to the design's full size when Spot, Down and the radial zones are all up, so you can read at a glance which sockets are giving the most light. The glow stays visible in Lit.
