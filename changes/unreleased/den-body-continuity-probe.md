target: internal
type: added

Operators can time each body-form dropout in the Den and attribute it to a cause, including perception stalls, with a read-only browser probe, host loopback tap and join script that keep aggregates and timestamps only. The cause with 60% or more of the blank time picks the flicker fix, and only from a window that met the occupancy gate.
