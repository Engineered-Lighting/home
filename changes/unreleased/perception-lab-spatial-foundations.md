---
title: Perception Lab spatial foundations for den depth, registration and zones
target: backend
type: added
---

Stereo room positions now measure depth age and skew against the associated RGB observation and expire with the source observation (never beyond three seconds), so realistic 1-3 s specialist latency no longer discards every depth sample; age and skew limits are configurable but can only tighten. Position uncertainty is the floor-plane spread of the measured support pixels plus sensor error instead of a 3D box-corner radius, the point stays a camera-facing visible surface, and only server-derived clock-mapped depth is trusted as validated. Recorded depth bundles carry a session-independent calibration content id, so a camera restart no longer invalidates a physical validation. New local modules map depth frames onto the causal RGB clock, associate RGB person boxes, torsos or masks with rectified-left depth through a validated reprojection, and publish RGB floor-contact positions for floor-projection arms only from supported ankle keypoints; a box bottom, which may be a desk or sofa edge, abstains. An offline operator tool (`tools/perception-lab-spatial-validate.py`) validates a three-zone den map, fits the camera-to-room registration, and evaluates at least 20 reference positions (median 0.25 m, p95 0.50 m) into content-addressed receipts. Estimates and depth ranges are always recomputed from recorded frames and detector output; typed values are refused. Each method must cover near, mid and far and every zone boundary with its own measured positions, with no confidently wrong zone decision and at most 25% abstentions. Only passed receipts validate the records, a failed attempt vetoes what it covers, error bounds cannot be edited after fitting, and a moved camera needs a new walkthrough. None of this is wired into the deployed Lab yet.
