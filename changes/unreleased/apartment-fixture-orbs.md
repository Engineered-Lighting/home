---
title: Den fixtures drawn as glowing frosted-glass cylinders
target: web
type: changed
---

Each modeled den fixture is now a 6 in × 3 in cylinder of frosted white glass, not an orb. Where its channels are on, it glows from inside: each radial zone on its side, the downlight on its base. The light blooms softly past the glass, and a small glow, seen through the frosted base from above, marks where the spot's beam starts. A held fixture gets an ice collar, and a spot aimed and waiting in the dark gets a dashed warm one. The flat square and floor line that the socket's survey marker drew under each fixture are gone. When the coordinator dims the room glow, each fixture's zones and wall washes now fade with it. Spot-only lights are small glass orbs. Picking, aiming, beams, channel levels and the light cast are unchanged, and nothing is sent to hardware.
