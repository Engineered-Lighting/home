Den fixture adoption

A clickable mockup of adopting the robotic spotlights into Home's den, one socket at a time. The room, names and readings are invented. Nothing here talks to hardware.

Try

  • New fixture found · Adopt in the room's capsule starts the main flow, into F3.
  • Each step has a Try line with its failures: not commissioned, a different bulb out, limits past the fixture's, and more.
  • After adopting, open F3 for its states and its Go live card. The phase after the go can be previewed there.
  • The Mix switch shows 1 + 3 to 4 + 0. Bulbs shows Hue as it would set, or as set.

Each socket, by kind

    What stays off

    • Adopting needs no motion and no lit spot. The socket ends adopted · virtual until the go.
    • The bench comes first, before the fixture goes up. Test moves then wait for the transport (your go) and the socket's own switch, which den-live's sender enforces.
    • The spot lights only after its aim check. Until a fixture is live, its room light stands in for the bulb it replaced.
    • Stop works everywhere. Resume is always your press.
