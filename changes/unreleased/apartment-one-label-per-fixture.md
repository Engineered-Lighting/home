Den fixtures in the Apartment room no longer show two stacked labels. While the room's clickable fixture labels are shown, the older canvas name tag under each fixture is hidden; cameras and other equipment without a room label keep their tags.

When the room labels retire, their canvas fallback returns immediately, keeping paused views and frame captures consistent across renderers.
