---
title: The aim reticle follows the pointer, and grabbing it switches to Spot
target: desktop
type: fixed
---

While you drag a Den light's aim reticle it now stays under the pointer. Home's button press effect used to park it at the top-left of the room until you let go. The reticle also shows while Zones is active: grabbing it switches that light to Spot and the Aim tab and aims in the same drag, with no separate click on Spot. If the pointer briefly lands on a surface the light can't reach, such as the top of the ceiling in the dollhouse view, the proposal stays on the last floor spot instead of jumping. The proposal still waits for Set aim or Discard, and nothing is sent to hardware.
