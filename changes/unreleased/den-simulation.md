title: Add a loopback den simulation that runs the pipeline tools on rendered takes
target: internal
type: added
---

Adds `tools/den-sim`, a local harness that serves the unchanged Home on `127.0.0.1`. A rendered, artificial person is recorded once through the real take, check and camera-registration tools, and a real detector, the Lab's YOLO26s and RTMPose-m workers run by the new `tools/den-detect` on this PC's CPU, records its rows beside each take, checked again against the model before the set is used. Each click re-checks that take and its rows by hash, runs the pinned observe, track and bundle tools on the rows, rebuilds each router request's exact Qwen request from the take's own pixels, and looks Qwen's reply up by that request's hash. Qwen's words are recorded once, by the owner's own paste of `tools/den-sim/ask_la.py`, which asks Qwen on the inference host about the requests still owed and files the replies on this PC; nothing on a click reaches the inference host, and until the paste a click stops at the reply with "Qwen has not seen these exact frames", while the page shows what Qwen still owes and the paste line. Need v0 runs on those replies, and the older lighting brain swings a virtual spotlight, with the fixture command shown on a fictional profile and marked not sent. At page start the couch, the owner's sofa mark or a declared stand-in seat, is checked with Home's own validator and written with its spot into the pipeline's own room model, and on each click `tools/den-sim/person.py` writes person states in the architecture's field names, running need v0 once per track, and tests whether the person is on the couch. A person card shows the distance to the seat point, on the couch or not, and the spot by name, and says that the older brain still decides the beam until the coordinator replaces it. A "Run every scene" button fills a plain table of all five scenes. The panel draws the detector's box and joints on the take's own frame, and shows the stills each Qwen request sends with every field of the reply. Each hop's word comes from a receipt the click writes, and the top line says plainly that the chain is not yet connected start to finish, and nothing has a route to a bulb, the camera, the Jetson or Home Assistant. Its data lives under `%USERPROFILE%\den-sim`, and the detector's venv under `%USERPROFILE%\ml\den-detector`.
