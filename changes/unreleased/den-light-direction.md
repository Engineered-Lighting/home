---
title: The den spot is directed, not switched
target: backend
type: changed
---

The den spotlight preview (`tools/den-live/preview.py`) now plays every change of its light decision the way a director of photography would: it uses the same FC13 commands a real C12 fixture executes, and Home can draw the same motion. The rules for when a spot lights are unchanged. The new `direction.py` decides how each change looks.
- **It blooms on.** A new task light rises over 2 s on a quarter-sine curve: visible with the yes itself, and half way at a third of its time. The fade curves are the C12's own (ease_out, ease_in_out, linear, as `c12.move` names them), so a fade played as a train is the one a board with the timed MOVE plays from a single command. The board has no fade, so the rise is a level train: one stationary MOVE each time the 0–254 level changes. A virtual fixture sends at most one a 0.1 s tick. A real one sends at most one every 0.5 s, the firmware's 2-commands-a-second rule; the steps renew the lease themselves, so no keep-lit goes between them. The light Home shows is always the last MOVE the trace committed.
- **One MOVE per cue on a board that can fade.** Where `c12.get_profile` advertises the timed MOVE (firmware v2-b and later) and the codec can encode it:
  - each fade is one MOVE carrying `fade_ms` and its curve;
  - a dark move followed by a rise is one MOVE that blooms on arrival;
  - an exit during a glide joins the flight;
  - a lit glide is stretched to a camera move's pace (below).

  The published light follows the board's own fade. Virtual fixtures and boards without it, such as Light 1 on v2-a, keep the capped level train.
- **It moves like a camera.** The aim is the page, not the wrists. A book is read square to the line of sight and held by its lower half, so the aim is 5 cm up the page from the hands (`page_up_m`): ahead of and above them at the chest, ahead of them in the lap, never back toward the face. A settled median replaces den-core's first-sample shift filter. A lit spot holds its frame while the page keeps 96% of the beam's centre light, so nothing moves while you read and a page turn never moves it. When you've really moved, it reframes once, after the book is still, at a camera move's pace. A real shift gets a dolly's 2 s (`glide_s`); a small correction creeps over 4 s (`creep_s`), slow enough not to catch the eye. The fixture's own S-curve is stretched to that time, so it starts and lands softly. Virtual fixtures fly it too, so Home draws the same move. In the reading sim that's about one lit move a session, the reframe after the reader's shift. Each glide is published with the S-curve the board flies (`glide`: from, to, start, duration and path limits). The S-curve is the firmware's own (its duration.py at 62ea043, with a separate deceleration limit and the timed MOVE's exact time-stretch), checked against all 36 of its planner vectors.
- **It doesn't flip-flop.** A seated reader's facing is averaged over 3 s, so a side-on fixture isn't flipped in and out of "in front of you" by the shoulders' frame-to-frame swing. A lit fixture keeps its job while its beam's centre clears the head by 12 cm (admission still needs 15 cm), and however the reader turns: turning to face a light that's on is fine (the owner's word). Only admission is den-core's side-on rule, so nothing new lights in front of the reader. A fixture already pre-aimed keeps the pre-aim. While a spot is lit, the runner-up waits on the same page, dark, so a hand-off is a 1.2 s crossfade and never a dark move. A crossfade keeps the page's light whole: den-core's dimming curve gives 18% of the light at level 0.5, so two ramps eased in levels would leave the page with 37% half way. A level train eases the light itself. A board with the timed MOVE uses its own curves, one MOVE each: the new fixture rises on ease_out while the old falls on ease_in_out over twice that, which never sums below the page's whole (at most 23% over, half way).
- **On a board without lit travel** (Light 1's v2-c until its lit bench test), every re-aim blanks the spot for the whole move and its settle. So the aim holds while the page stays within half of half the beam's width (84% of the centre's light). When the page has gone, a standby that has already landed on it takes the job as a crossfade. Only with no standby does the spot fade down, move dark and rise. A real board arrives after its settle (`settle_s`, which the firmware's `predicted_ms` counts), so a dark move relights no sooner.
- **Eye safety is a floor, not the show.** den-core's eye rule decides what may light. On top of it, a beam whose centre actually crosses a head is cut at once: the reader's head and each eye at 12 cm, and anyone else's head, checked on the beam as it really points (a rise in place, a held frame or a standby's hand-over can point a few degrees off the line den-core checks). A timed MOVE flying dark is judged where it will light. A fixture is admitted only at den-core's 15 cm on the beam as it will be aimed, and a fixture that was cut waits 1.5 s before it lights again.
- **A refusal for the beam through the head, or for no head to check, cuts at once**, in one level-0 MOVE, whatever fade the light is in. Easing is only for ordinary hand-offs and exits.
- **The radio rule:** a real fixture is never sent more than 2 commands in any second, keep-lit and Stop counted, and the direction's own commands, an exit's steps included, take at most one of them, so a cut or a Stop never has to be the third (a third is queued behind the others, not refused). Stop and a release (dark at once) never wait. An exit ends inside the board's lease from the last lit MOVE or keep-lit.
- **It leaves gracefully.** Standing up fades out in 0.3 s, dark within 1 s of standing. On a real board it goes dark at once (`leave_fade_board_s` 0): detection and the send path take about 0.9 s, which leaves plan 05's bar C (dark within 1.0 s on 4 of 5) no room for a fade. A task that ends while you're still seated (an answer, the phone patience) fades out over 1 s and is dark at its deadline. A hand-off crossfades. No exit keeps a beam on past the validity a snapshot already promised. Unchanged and still instant: Stop, Off, expiry, a stale or unsafe snapshot, and another person binding. A fixture in the middle of a glide gets one level-0 MOVE to its own target, because the board holds a MOVE that arrives during a move until it lands.
- **The room follows the spot.** The snapshot gains `ambient`. It carries the room glow now (`level`, `scale`, `cct_k`) and the cue it's on (`goal_level`, `goal_scale`, `goal_cct_k`, `phase`, `ramp_s` and `ramp_starts_mono`), so a bulb that fades itself can take one plan per cue. Under a task light, the room's glow dims to 40% (and warms to 2700 K) over 4 s, starting a beat after the spot. It comes back over 1.5 s as the spot leaves. It's published only: nothing is written to Home Assistant, and the real Hue lights are untouched.
- **New settings,** all changeable from Home: `fade_in_s` 2.0, `fade_out_s` 1.0, `leave_fade_s` 0.3 (`leave_fade_board_s` 0 on a real board), `crossfade_s` 1.2, `shift_m` 0.06, `shift_hold_s` 1.5, `page_up_m` 0.05, and the ambient levels and times.
- **Before and after**, on a synthetic reading session at the camera's live rate, lag and ask pace: sit, open the book (at the chest or in the lap), three page turns, a 35 cm shift along the sofa, stand. 60 runs per case (20 seeds, 3 facings), with percentiles pooled over every lit sample. The page's true place is a family chosen apart from either rule (the centre of plausible grips, and 8 cm off it forward, sideways and up), because the round-3 page sat 3 cm from where the new rule looked. It is a model, not the room.
  - **The aim** is about as close to the page at the median as before (9 cm, chest or lap), and the page is inside the beam's bright middle as often (94–95%, against 93–95%). On round 3's own lap page it's 4.5 cm, as before, so the lap regression is gone. When the reader has moved, the spot reframes and the page is back in the middle every time (p95 8–10 cm). A board without lit travel holds the frame through the move instead, about 31 cm off at a far fixture with two thirds of the page's light, since each re-aim there blanks the spot. A book held in one hand isn't helped.
  - **The motion:** about one lit move a session, the reframe after the reader's 35 cm shift, at 2–2.5 s. Nothing moves while you read.
  - **The page's light** dips (below 70%) about 0.55 times a session, almost all of it the reader's own move, against 1.3–2.0 before. Cuts to dark while reading go from 0.8–1.8 a session to 0–0.04, and dark time from 0.25–0.66 s to 0–0.07 s, on a virtual fixture. A one-frame pose dropout still darkens at once, by the rule.
  - **Yes to first light:** 0.10 s at p50 on a virtual fixture (0.72 s before at the chest), 0.56 s at p95. On the real boards, 0.58 s at p50: the free radio slot makes the rise's MOVE wait out the second after the last dark pre-aim. Standing to dark: 0.86 s at p50, at most 1.06 s, on a virtual fixture. On a real board, with plan 05's 0.3 s send path added, 0.88 s at p50 and at most 1.06 s.
