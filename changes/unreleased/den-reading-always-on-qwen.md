Virtual reading qualification uses the incumbent always-on Qwen receipt bank
alongside compatible raw pose evidence. Only the same camera/capture and a
whole Qwen input window inside the continuous sole-reader interval qualify;
withheld, missing, expired, invalid or ambiguous evidence cannot silently gain
reading authority. Original expiry and household egress gates remain intact.
