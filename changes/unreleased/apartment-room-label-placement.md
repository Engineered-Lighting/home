---
title: Room labels no longer overlap each other or the room controls
target: desktop
type: fixed
---

In the Apartment room, fixture labels now stack clear of one another and of the Zones/Spot pill, the room toolbar, the hint bar and the legend, and stay inside the visible room on phones and tablets. Each label keeps its full 44 px touch area, and idle labels use a shorter form of the fixture's registered name when every label is shown.
