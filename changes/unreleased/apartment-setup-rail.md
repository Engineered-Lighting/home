---
title: Apartment Setup opens a tool rail with its inspector in the right column
target: desktop
type: changed
---

In the redesigned Apartment view, Setup now opens a left rail (Devices and sockets, Names, Named aims, Zones, Fixture position, Home Assistant, Cameras and sensors) with the chosen tool's inspector, Done and its saved state in the right column. Pressing Setup again leaves it, the Controls/Chat tabs and camera dock are hidden while it is open and the dock comes back exactly as it was, and camera calibration can be started from Cameras and sensors. Every existing editor saves exactly the same data as before; Victoria shows which tools apply to its registration and why the others are unavailable.
