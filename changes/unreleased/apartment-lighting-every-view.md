---
title: Lighting applies to every Apartment view
target: web
type: changed
---

Lit is no longer a separate fourth view. A Lighting toggle beside Cloud, Photo and Mesh relights whichever view is shown and stays on when you switch views. Photo and Cloud are relit from the scan's verified lighting layer, and Mesh is relit as before. When a view can't be lit (for example, the scan has no lighting layer yet), the toolbar says so in plain words. Lighting is still simulated with assumed output, and it is off by default.
