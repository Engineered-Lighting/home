A Den body frame may now be drawn for 2 s from its earliest possible capture, up from 1 s. On the den camera a frame is about half a second old when it's published, and about a second old when it reaches Home. With a 1 s lease, almost nothing could be drawn. Home must accept the 2 s lease before this ships.
