---
title: Chat's Auto home follows the home you're viewing
target: web
type: fixed
---

Light commands in Chat that name no home, such as "brighter" or "50%", now go
to the home shown in the Apartment view (or the home of an attached room or
fixture chip) before falling back to your location or the last home used. The
composer's Auto label shows that home with "the home you're viewing", and a
home picked by hand still wins. Existing Chat refusals are unchanged.
