---
title: Compose modeled ambient light around a fixture
target: desktop
type: added
---

Adds a six-direction ambient composer at the selected fixture, with Zones/Spot controls and a compact keyboard-accessible ring for brightness and warmth. Dragging updates levels immediately while the continuous light shape and diffuser ease smoothly; stationary clicks preserve levels as the camera settles, existing modeled channels, registered fixtures and spotlight aim are preserved, and nothing is sent to hardware.
