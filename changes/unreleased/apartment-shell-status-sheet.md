---
title: Home status names each system, keeps unsaved marks per home, and the phone sheet drags
target: web
type: changed
---

The Home status popover now has one row per system: room, lights, saved work,
camera and capabilities. Victoria says its Den fixtures have no light entities
and never shows Echo Park's Home Assistant state. Each home's switcher button
keeps a hollow mark while it holds unsaved work, including local drafts, even
when you are viewing the other home. Switching away says the work was kept. On
phones and tablets, drag the sheet handle between peek, half and full, or tap
it or use the arrow keys; the sheet eases over 0.22 s. Touch screens keep zoom,
turn and Fit buttons so the room never needs dragging. The keyboard now moves
from the header to the room toolbar before the side panel.
