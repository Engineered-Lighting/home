---
title: Lit view in the Apartment (simulated, not photometric)
target: web
type: added
---

The room toolbar's Lit button now relights the scan mesh with simulated light from the modeled fixtures, using their current Spot, Down and radial settings. Spot shadows are crisp and down shadows soft, under a fixed night exposure. The view is labelled "Simulated · not photometric · assumed output", because fixture output uses the design's assumed lumen ratings rather than measurements. Lit is off by default and turns off when you pick another view. If it cannot keep up, it says so and reduces its own quality.
