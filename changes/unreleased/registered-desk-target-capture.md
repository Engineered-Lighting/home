---
title: Select a desktop target from the registered scan
target: web
type: added
---

A bounded helper captures four owner-selected scan-surface corners and seals
their exact scan and equipment identities. The target remains an unqualified
scan selection with no camera, hardware or physical-light authority.

Home's existing canvas can retain this selection as a private download. An
owner-local consumer reuses the virtual fixture model to calculate aim candidates
with explicit angle conventions, separately from automatic lighting admission.

Moving focus to the whole-desktop confirmation checkbox preserves the selected
corners. Completed selections also survive download-dialog focus changes;
explicit cancellation and changed room/equipment authority still retire them.
