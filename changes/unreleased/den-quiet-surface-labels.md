---
title: Quieter labelled surfaces in the den view
target: web
type: changed
---

The den's labelled surfaces are now drawn by their corners alone, so they sit lightly on the point cloud. Only the
surfaces in use say their names: the ones near a person, and the one a lit spot lands on (marked with a warm dot).
Hovering a surface, or tapping it on a phone, draws its full outline and name. Layers has a new Room group to hide the
surfaces or show every name, and the Labels tool still shows the full boxes while it's open.
