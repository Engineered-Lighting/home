---
title: Record a partner from the People tab
target: web
type: added
---

The household card gains a control to record a partnership, choosing from the
people already visible to you.

It says plainly what it is doing: you are recording this yourself, and it is
stored as your account's statement about your household rather than as something
the other person confirmed. That is the honest description of an
owner-attested fact, and the record keeps the same distinction — the authority
axis says who had the standing, and the receipt's scope says whether you were
one end of the relationship or asserting about two other people.

After a successful attestation the card re-reads the relationships from Core
rather than patching itself locally, so it can never display a partnership Core
did not record. A failed attestation shows the error and changes nothing.
