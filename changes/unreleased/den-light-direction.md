---
title: The den spot is directed, not switched
target: backend
type: changed
---

The den spotlight preview (`tools/den-live/preview.py`) now plays every change of its light decision the way a director of photography would: it uses the same FC13 commands a real C12 fixture executes, and Home can draw the same motion. The rules for when a spot lights are unchanged. The new `direction.py` decides how each change looks.
- **It blooms on.** A new task light rises over 2 s on a quarter-sine curve: visible with the yes itself, and half way at a third of its time. The fade curves are the C12's own (ease_out, ease_in_out, linear, as `c12.move` names them), so a fade played as a train is the one a board with the timed MOVE plays from a single command. The board has no fade, so the rise is a level train: one stationary MOVE each time the 0–254 level changes. A virtual fixture sends at most one a 0.1 s tick. A real one sends at most one every 0.5 s, the firmware's 2-commands-a-second rule; the steps renew the lease themselves, so no keep-lit goes between them. The light Home shows is always the last MOVE the trace committed.
- **One MOVE per cue on a board that can fade.** Where `c12.get_profile` advertises the timed MOVE (firmware v2-b and later) and the codec can encode it:
  - each fade is one MOVE carrying `fade_ms` and its curve;
  - a dark move followed by a rise is one MOVE that blooms on arrival;
  - an exit during a glide joins the flight;
  - a lit glide takes a 1 s floor (`glide_min_s`): the same S-curve, slower.

  The published light follows the board's own fade. Virtual fixtures and boards without it, such as Light 1 on v2-a, keep the capped level train.
- **It glides once.** The aim is the page, not the wrists. A book is read square to the line of sight and held by its lower half, so the aim is 5 cm up the page from the hands (`page_up_m`): ahead of and above them at the chest, ahead of them in the lap, never back toward the face. A settled median replaces den-core's first-sample shift filter. A page turn never moves a lit spot. A real shift moves it once, as one lit MOVE, after the book comes to rest. Each glide is published with the S-curve the board flies (`glide`: from, to, start, duration and path limits). The S-curve is the firmware's own (its duration.py at 62ea043, with a separate deceleration limit and the timed MOVE's exact time-stretch), checked against all 36 of its planner vectors.
- **It doesn't flip-flop.** A seated reader's facing is averaged over 3 s, so a side-on fixture isn't flipped in and out of "in front of you" by the shoulders' frame-to-frame swing. A lit fixture keeps its job while its beam's centre clears the head by 12 cm (admission still needs 15 cm), and while it's no more than 15° in front of side-on (admission is still den-core's side-on, and den-core alone keeps to 5°). A fixture already pre-aimed keeps the pre-aim. While a spot is lit, the runner-up waits on the same page, dark, so a hand-off is a 1.2 s crossfade and never a dark move. The crossfade eases the light, not the level: den-core's dimming curve gives 18% of the light at level 0.5, so two ramps eased in levels would leave the page with 37% half way. A board with the timed MOVE plays a crossfade as a level train, because its own curves act on levels.
- **On a board without lit travel** (Light 1's v2-c until its lit bench test), every re-aim blanks the spot for the whole move and its settle. So the aim holds while the page stays within half of half the beam's width (84% of the centre's light). When the page has gone, a standby that has already landed on it takes the job as a crossfade. Only with no standby does the spot fade down, move dark and rise. A real board arrives after its settle (`settle_s`, which the firmware's `predicted_ms` counts), so a dark move relights no sooner.
- **The radio rule:** a real fixture is never sent more than 2 commands in any second, keep-lit and Stop counted. The direction's own commands wait their turn. Stop, a release (dark at once) and a leaving exit's fade never wait.
- **It leaves gracefully.** Standing up fades out in 0.3 s, dark within 1 s of standing. A task that ends while you're still seated (an answer, the phone patience) fades out over 1 s and is dark at its deadline. A hand-off crossfades. No exit keeps a beam on past the validity a snapshot already promised. Unchanged and still instant: Stop, Off, expiry, a stale or unsafe snapshot, and another person binding. A fixture in the middle of a glide gets one level-0 MOVE to its own target, because the board holds a MOVE that arrives during a move until it lands.
- **The room follows the spot.** The snapshot gains `ambient`. It carries the room glow now (`level`, `scale`, `cct_k`) and the cue it's on (`goal_level`, `goal_scale`, `goal_cct_k`, `phase`, `ramp_s` and `ramp_starts_mono`), so a bulb that fades itself can take one plan per cue. Under a task light, the room's glow dims to 40% (and warms to 2700 K) over 4 s, starting a beat after the spot. It comes back over 1.5 s as the spot leaves. It's published only: nothing is written to Home Assistant, and the real Hue lights are untouched.
- **New settings,** all changeable from Home: `fade_in_s` 2.0, `fade_out_s` 1.0, `leave_fade_s` 0.3, `crossfade_s` 1.2, `shift_m` 0.06, `shift_hold_s` 1.5, `page_up_m` 0.05, and the ambient levels and times.
- **Before and after**, on a synthetic reading session at the camera's live rate, lag and ask pace: sit, open the book (at the chest or in the lap), three page turns, a 35 cm shift along the sofa, stand. 60 runs per case (20 seeds, 3 facings), with percentiles pooled over every lit sample. The page's true place is a family chosen apart from either rule (the centre of plausible grips, and 8 cm off it forward, sideways and up), because the round-3 page sat 3 cm from where the new rule looked. It is a model, not the room.
  - **The aim** is about as close to the page at the median as before (9 cm either way, chest or lap), but the tail is shorter: the pooled p95 goes from 25 to 19 cm at the chest and from 22 to 18 cm in the lap. Within half the beam's width: from 93–95% to 96%. The lap regression the re-check found is gone: on round 3's own lap page, 4.4 cm, against the old rule's 4.5 cm.
  - **The page's light** (any dip below 70% counts) dips 0.5–0.6 times a session with lit travel, against 1.3–2.0 before, and about once on a board without lit travel, against 1.8–2.8. Every one of those dips is the reader's own 35 cm move: the spot follows once the book is still. Hand-offs from pose noise go from 0.97 a session to 0.03. A one-frame pose dropout still darkens at once, by the rule.
  - **Yes to first light:** 0.06 s at p50 at the chest (0.72 s before), and 0.56 s at p95 on a virtual fixture. On a real board the tail is longer (p95 0.96 s): a pre-aim lands after its settle, and noise can by then have every side-on socket just in front of the reader. Standing to dark: 0.86 s at p50, at most 1.06 s.
