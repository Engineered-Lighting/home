---
title: Show the whole measured person in the Den figure when the owner chooses
target: backend
type: changed
---

An owner body profile can set `"head": "shown"`. The figure then keeps every measured depth point of the person, including the head, with no head cut and no face envelope. That profile needs no head-cut qualification record to install. It still binds to the camera registration and the camera's capture-age receipt. A profile without the setting keeps the head cut, exactly as before.
