---
title: Map named aims in Setup by clicking a surface, with in-room handles
target: desktop
type: added
---

Setup › Named aims now has New: click a desk top, the painting, a wall or the floor and the aim drops there, fitting furniture tops and art to their own size and suggesting a name and type. Reach says which aimable lights can reach it and why the others can't, Preview from a light draws its beam, and Save is offered only for a unique name and a type that matches the surface. In the room, drag a corner to resize it, the center to move it within its plane and, on flat surfaces, the arrow to raise or lower it with a line dropping to the floor. Clicking an aim reopens it, Escape steps back, and Delete removes the name while lights keep their current aim. Named aims are geometry only; nothing is sent to a light.
