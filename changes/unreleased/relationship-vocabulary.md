---
title: Friends, Siblings, Roommates, Neighbours and Colleagues Can Be Recorded
target: backend
type: added
---

Until now the only relationships the system would accept were "parent of" and "partnered with". Every other kind of connection in a household — friends, siblings, roommates, neighbours, colleagues — could not be recorded at all, even though the People tab had always described people that way.

All five can now be recorded alongside the two that already worked.

Each of the new ones applies in both directions, the way "partnered with" already did: recording that one person is another's friend records the friendship, not a one-way claim. Only "parent of" reads differently depending on which way round you read it.
