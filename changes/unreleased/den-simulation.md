Adds `tools/den-sim`, a local harness that serves the unchanged Home on `127.0.0.1`. A rendered, artificial person is recorded once through the real take, check and camera-registration tools; each click re-checks that take by hash, runs the pinned observe, track, bundle and need tools on it, and the older lighting brain swings a virtual spotlight, with the fixture command shown on a fictional profile and marked not sent. Each hop's word comes from a receipt the click writes, and the top line says plainly that the chain is not yet connected start to finish: no inference runs, and nothing has a route to a bulb, the camera, the Jetson, the inference host or Home Assistant. Its data lives under `%USERPROFILE%\den-sim`.
