In Echo Park's room view, ceiling lights are drawn as recessed discs with a rim, tinted by their colour temperature and brightness when on, and plug lamps as small spheres that glow warm when on. A light with the co-located Engineered profile adds a small white cylinder labelled "simulated". Selecting, hovering and controlling lights work as before.
