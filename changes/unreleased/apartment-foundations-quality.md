---
title: Readable, touch-sized Home controls in both themes
target: web
type: fixed
---

In the unified Home, every status badge now uses one pill style with sans
text, a spinning loading marker and a glowing live dot. A new "Holding" badge
shows the virtual preview's hold exactly as the preview reports it. In light
theme the room and its toolbar, popovers and notices stay dark and readable,
while the column follows the theme with darker secondary text. On touch
screens the status summary, sheet handle, toolbar buttons, sliders, checkbox
rows, radial zones and Chat controls are at least 44 px. Small or faint
informative text in the Chat setup, Setup editor and keyboard navigator is
raised to 11.5 px and readable contrast. An authored browser audit checks
contrast, touch targets, surfaces, motion and the phone layout for both homes
in both themes.
