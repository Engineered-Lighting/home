Echo ceiling lights and plugs now open the same Light inspector as the Den, with power, brightness and colour temperature where Home Assistant supports them, the entity line, and disabled controls with the last time Home Assistant reported when it is offline. Echo's room view adds Quick All on, All off and chips for the home's Home Assistant scenes and scripts; every Quick action and room switch, including the Den's modeled ones, offers Undo that restores the exact previous state, and Travel Mode or an offline Home Assistant refuses them with a reason. Room rows summarise each light, Den channel sliders turn their channel on, and the selected Den light shows a thin axis and faint cone, with Down pools labelled modeled.
