---
title: Add engineered Apartment light aiming previews
target: web
type: added
---

Apartment now supports explicit engineered-gimbal adoption, named and exact-mesh aim previews, surface-plane-aligned FWHM beam footprints, read-only desktop telemetry, and capability-aware Home Assistant spotlight and six-zone mappings. The simulation can evaluate those controls over a read-only snapshot of the locally saved Apartment layout, with a compact Lighting inspector, direct fixture-to-inspector selection, an explicit preview-to-current "Set simulated aim" action with a reduced-motion-aware beam sweep, stable current aim while a new destination is only being previewed, persistent Kelvin-aware beams, dotted off-state cones, a convex-mirror soft-wash preview, and direct radial-zone controls in both the inspector and 3D view. Physical gimbal movement remains disabled until the owner-authority and coordinate-frame contracts exist.
