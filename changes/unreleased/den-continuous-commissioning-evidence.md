---
title: Preserve historical baselines while commissioning complete Den monitoring
target: backend
type: fixed
---

Den monitoring can read a separately reviewed first-scope baseline without
replacing or extending any scope already recorded. Continuous operation can
require fresh, successful automatic-media-cleanup evidence; missing or failed
cleanup latches the existing stop mechanism. A Jetson system firmware-metadata
refresh failure remains visible as an unrelated maintenance notice; user-scope,
unknown service and hardware failures retain their admission checks.
