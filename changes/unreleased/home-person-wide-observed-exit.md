Commissioned person-wide absence or departure can advance the normal observed exit delay for that exact person's existing task episodes. Lifecycle proofs retain same-source historical observation ancestry when ordinary newer observations replace the earlier head; source withdrawal and proof expiry still fence them. Missing observations, identity resets and unqualified coverage cannot end an episode or create a new activity.
