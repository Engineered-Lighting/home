The Apartment room toolbar now lists every layer, including unavailable and planned ones with the reason each is off, and offers an on-demand legend of the room's marks. Press ? for a gestures-and-keys list that matches the keys the room binds, and open "What's real here" to see what is live, modeled, authored or recorded in the current home. The legend choice lasts for the browser session.
