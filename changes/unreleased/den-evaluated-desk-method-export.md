The opt-in local desk Guide freezes independent method inputs, records a selected-anchor measurement, then resolves published exposure originals before computing error, containment, missingness and latency against frozen requirements. A private commissioning export requires separately qualified originals and the existing strict consumer; missing qualification and the new bundled candidate family keep export unavailable.

An explicit diagnostic successor can measure aligned anchor residuals while the method's error bound and hidden coverage remain unknown. It preserves unknown service/protection metrics and blocks export rather than turning measured residuals into qualification.
