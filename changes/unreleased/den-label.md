title: Den label tool for the N1 single-person reference
target: internal
type: added
---

`tools/den-label` lets the owner hand-label retained staged den takes on the
PC: person boxes on keyframes with an audit, then engagement intervals with
demand bands, under an annotation protocol (`PROTOCOL-V1.md`) committed before
any label. It splits sessions by rule, re-marks one session, and freezes the
input fixture and the labels separately with their own hashes. Frames and
labels stay on the PC; only freeze lines with hashes and counts reach git.
