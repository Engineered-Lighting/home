An explicitly commissioned successor profile supports durable scene-scoped
brightness corrections and attributed clearance of exact protection hazards.
Home separates accepted requests from their applied or rejected outcomes.
Hazards and immutable resolutions survive restart; missing protection still
withdraws virtual light. New sessions require explicit history reconciliation.
Historical profiles and physical writer restrictions retain their meaning.
