---
title: Den movement-intent bridge holds back clips the model worker would refuse and reports its drops
target: backend
type: fixed
---

The Den bridge no longer samples frames the optional V-JEPA-LM worker contract refuses: frames without a finite camera device time on every plane, frames outside RGB mode or with an invalid session name are skipped and counted, never re-timed. A track binding the worker cannot admit, such as one without a scan revision, is neither advertised nor sampled. A cancel the worker does not recognise is counted once. While the bridge is enabled, the journal status line shows its sent, hint, expired, rejected and unknown counts, its skipped frames and refused bindings, and its last drop reason.
