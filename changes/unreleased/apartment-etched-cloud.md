---
title: Apartment Cloud view drawn as an etching
target: desktop
type: changed
---

The Apartment's Cloud view now draws the scan after the engineered.lighting site: walls and floors read as even
planes of fine points, edges, corners and outlines are drawn brighter on top, and near walls hide the rooms behind
them. It applies on desktop WebGL once the scan has loaded; phones and the WebGPU renderer keep the classic cloud.
Set localStorage `apartment3d.pointLook` to `classic` to keep the classic cloud in a browser.
