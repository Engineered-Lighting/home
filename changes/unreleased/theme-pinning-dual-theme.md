---
title: The paper theme works everywhere it should; immersive views commit to dark
target: web
type: fixed
---

The apartment 3D suite and video labeler are now deliberately dark (their
scenes and charts are built for it) — previously flipping to the light
"paper" theme rendered their token-colored text as ink on near-black. Every
other surface becomes genuinely dual-theme: the feature-loading panel, the
metrics-lab chart (its white-painted bars, outlines, and axis labels now use
theme ink with identical dark rendering), and the Lights drawer's hardcoded
status oranges/reds now come from tokens. The browser chrome color follows
the active theme, the page body no longer flashes black behind the paper
theme, and boot-failure diagnostics plus AI-stack failure logs are finally
selectable for copy-paste.
