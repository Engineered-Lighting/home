---
title: Bind Home chat to the room
target: desktop
type: added
---

In the redesigned Home view, lights and named aims that a reply names appear as chips beneath it. Hovering a chip highlights it in the room where a highlight exists, and clicking a light selects it. Action cards get Show and Adjust, and replies from the other home read as unavailable. With a light selected, the composer shows a removable "About" chip. A message with that chip is answered locally and is never sent to the model, Home Assistant or an external provider: brighter, dimmer, warmer, cooler or a level like 50% changes only the selected Victoria light in the room model, and Los Angeles changes are refused. The chat header shows the model and what Chat draws on in the current home, and the progress line shows the first tool step. Stop now works before the first reply text too, and it says whether a tool step had started.
