---
title: Record Relationships Between Two Other People from the People Tab
target: web
type: added
---

The People tab can now record who is whose parent, and who is partnered with whom, for people other than yourself. Previously it offered only "record my partner", so the rest of a household could not be described from the app at all even though the system had accepted such records for some time.

Pick a person, pick the relationship, pick the other person. The list of relationships is fixed to the two the system recognises rather than free text. Choosing the same person on both sides is not offered, and would be refused anyway.

As with recording your own partner, this is stored as your account's statement about your household — never as something either person confirmed.
