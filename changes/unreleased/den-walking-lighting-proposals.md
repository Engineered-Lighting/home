Den Live can publish opt-in walking-path proposals using bound motion forecasts, fixture travel and settling time, conservative floor and head clearance, and the ordinary command cadence. Walking remains separate from seated reading and physical fixture commands; manual holds and Stop take priority.

The realtime evidence identifies the actual target source, original expiry, timing assumptions and selected or ignored intent. Semantic hints remain advisory, and virtual proposals do not claim commissioned geometry, calibrated uncertainty or physical clock qualification.
